01 — The restaurant

On the harbour of Ouistreham, a table open every day, at lunch and in the evening. Come for lunch just steps from the boats, or dine as the sky changes colour.

1 place du Général de Gaulle, in the heart of the harbour. A brasserie menu, from land and sea — set menus, starters, mains, desserts, drinks and wines — to explore at your own pace.
